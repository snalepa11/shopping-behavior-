## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?

- What are the most popular products?
  The most popular product is leggings, followed by dresses
- Are more seasons more profitable than others?
  Spring is the most profitable season for this company
- Is the data complete and reliable?
- Are there biases or limitations in the dataset?
- Were the PROMO codes effective?
  Most purchases were made without the use of a PROMO code. However, there is a high frequency of purchases where a PROMO code is used. Therefore, I would say the PROMO codes were effective.
- Do more men or women buy products from this website?
  It seems that there is a realtively equal split of men and women who buy items form this website, but there are slightly more men and a very small amount of non-binary people buy items from this website.

## Summary of Findings

Several key findings came to light in the course of this investigation:

1. The top three products are shorts, sunglasses, and running shoes. This findaing can potentially be related to the most popular purchasing season is Spring.
2. We can also see that the store is equally as popular with men and women as there is a near equal split of men and women who shop at this store, with only a slightly increased number of male shoppers.
3. It is also apparent that promocodes may not be as effective as desired as less than half of purchses were wihtout the use of a promo code
